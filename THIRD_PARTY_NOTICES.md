## Online music tools

CAS launches these tools as separate executable processes. They are included in
`resources/online-tools/<platform>-<arch>`; the pinned release URLs and SHA-256
checksums are recorded in `scripts/online-tools.json` in the CAS source repository.
No user-installed Python, Node, Deno or FFmpeg is required.
